## What's new in v0.2.4

- Leaving the meeting page no longer stops the recording. Browse anywhere in the app while the meeting keeps transcribing; a floating pill shows the live waveform and timer, jumps back to the meeting on click, and can stop it from anywhere.
- New Granola-style floating widget: while recording, a small always-on-top pill floats above your other windows — even when ContextBrain is in the background — with the timer, live waveform, and stop control.
